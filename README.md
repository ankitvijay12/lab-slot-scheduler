# Lab Equipment & Slot Scheduler

> A centralized lab slot reservation and equipment tracking web application designed to eliminate scheduling conflicts and manual logbook queues in university computer and hardware labs.

---

## 💡 The Problem & Solution
Engineering students frequently face scheduling conflicts and long wait times due to manual logbook tracking and poor visibility of available hardware or computer systems in departmental labs. We are building a centralized lab slot reservation and equipment tracking web application that allows students to check real-time system availability, book time slots in advance, and enables lab assistants to monitor equipment usage efficiently, eliminating queues and administrative overhead.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
* **Frontend:** React, Vite, Tailwind CSS
* **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js
* **Database:** MySQL
* **APIs/Services:** JWT Authentication
* **Deployment:** Vercel & Render
